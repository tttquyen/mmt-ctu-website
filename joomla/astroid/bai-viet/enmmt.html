Introduction

The Faculty of Computer Networks and Communications was established in 1998 as the Department of Computer Systems and Communications. In 2009 it was renamed the Department of Computer Networking and Communication. On 29 September 2022, the department became the Faculty of Computer Networks and Communications following the resolution of the Council of Can Tho University.

The Faculty's staff is highly qualified — 02 Associate Professors, 07 PhDs, 08 Masters and 01 Engineer — most of whom graduated from countries with advanced education systems. The Faculty is in charge of undergraduate programmes in the field of computer networks and communications. Faculty members also assist in teaching, evaluating training programmes, and supervising master's students and PhD candidates at other universities in Can Tho and the provinces of the Mekong Delta.

Our members are actively involved in scientific research with significant sources of research funding both on and off campus. We have experience in participating in, consulting on and managing research projects at different levels, many of which have been applied in practice. Faculty members and students also contribute through books, book chapters and articles published in prestigious ISI/Scopus-indexed journals and conferences.

The Faculty members are dedicated to imparting knowledge and skills in the field of computer networks and communications, while equipping students with good values and professional attitudes to serve the community.
