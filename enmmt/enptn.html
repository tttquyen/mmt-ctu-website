Research laboratories

Modern facilities, continuously invested in and upgraded to improve the learning experience and the research capability of lecturers and students.

The Faculty of Computer Networks and Communications is fully equipped with modern facilities that meet the needs of teaching and scientific research. These facilities are continuously invested in and upgraded to improve the learning experience and the research capability of lecturers and students.

The Faculty runs two research laboratories:

Computer Networks, Mobile Communications & Big Data Lab

Three focus areas: computer networks, high-performance computing and data science. Head of laboratory: Assoc. Prof. Dr. Do Thanh Nghi.

View details

Information Security Lab

A practical environment for attack and defence techniques, penetration testing, malware analysis and digital forensics supporting the Information Security programme.

Library and learning resources

The Learning Resource Center: 7,560 m², about 1,000 seats, 357 computers, 158,527 print titles and 170,397 electronic resources, open 7:00–21:00. The College library: 180 m², 54 seats, about 3,529 specialised titles and journals.

Network and information systems

10 Gb/s backbone; 650 Mbps international and 6 Gbps domestic Internet; 803 Wi-Fi access points campus-wide, 20 high-speed points at the College; academic management system, Moodle, Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 for every student.

Makerspace

The MIS-CTU makerspace on the College campus, plus self-study and group-work areas with power outlets and Wi-Fi.

Safety and accessibility

24/7 security, CCTV and automatic fire alarms; new buildings have ramps, lifts and accessible toilets.
