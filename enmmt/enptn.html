Intelligent Computing, Big Data, Cloud Computing and Information Security Laboratory

The laboratory of the Faculty of Computer Networks and Communications, supporting hands-on teaching and research for lecturers and students. Head of laboratory: Assoc. Prof. Dr. Do Thanh Nghi.

The laboratory brings the Faculty's research and practical work together in one unit: intelligent computing, big data, cloud computing and information security. It operates CitCloud, the private cloud of the College of Information and Communication Technology, which provides a specialised environment for practicals and research in both programmes — Computer Networks and Data Communications, and Information Security.

Area 01

Intelligent computing

Machine learning, deep learning and artificial intelligence applied to recognition, recommendation and network attack detection.

Area 02

Big data

Big data processing and classification, high-performance computing, and parallel and distributed algorithms on Spark.

Area 03

Cloud computing

The CitCloud private cloud, microservice architectures, computer networks, the Internet of Things and distributed systems.

Area 04

Information security

Network security, intrusion detection, malware analysis, computer forensics, attack and defence techniques and web application security.

Research that feeds teaching

Lecturers' research results flow into teaching through updated course content, reading lists and graduation thesis topics. Leading directions — network security and AI-based intrusion detection, big data and Spark, cloud computing and microservices, the Internet of Things and machine learning — map directly onto courses in the curriculum.

Specialised courses such as Big Data Processing, Big Data Classification, Network Attack Detection, Computer Forensics and Website Security are practised on the laboratory's equipment and infrastructure. From their second or third year, students can join research groups and carry out projects and student research in the laboratory (see research activities).

CitCloud cloud infrastructure

The laboratory has deployed CitCloud, the private cloud of the College of ICT (cloud.cit.ctu.edu.vn/horizon), running on six high-end servers and providing a specialised environment for practicals and research. Several specialised courses use CitCloud virtual machines alongside the computer labs.

Requesting virtual machines on CitCloud

CitCloud provides Ubuntu Server virtual machines for lab work, research and specialised projects. Windows machines are issued only when the user supplies the software licence. Requests are served in this order: teaching support (e.g. servers for web or network programming labs), research, College projects, then technology-transfer projects.

  1. RequestFill in the form: name, e-mail, unit, purpose, period of use, configuration (CPU, RAM, disk) and number of machines.
  2. ReviewThe laboratory assistant checks system capacity.
  3. ApprovalThe head of laboratory or the Faculty leadership confirms the request.
  4. ProvisioningThe user receives an IP address and an account and connects via SSH (Linux) or PuTTY (Windows); allocations are published.
  • Use the machine only for the stated purpose; change the password on receipt and take responsibility for security and licences.
  • CitCloud provides computing for the registered period only, not long-term storage.
  • Exposing services to the Internet requires permission from the College leadership.

Classrooms and floor space

Can Tho University has 492 classrooms and lecture halls (40–280 seats) and 233,274.72 m² of floor space — 6.2 m² per student against the required 2.8 m². The College of ICT also has 3 conference rooms and 18 function rooms; a new 10,600 m² building is under way for 2025–2028.

Library and learning resources

The Learning Resource Center and 14 branch libraries: 170,397 digital resources, 158,527 print titles (334,278 copies), 23 reading rooms with about 1,000 seats and 357 computers; 23 licensed databases (ScienceDirect, SpringerLink, IEEE Xplore, ProQuest…) and 44 open-access databases. The College library holds about 2,270 titles (3,529 copies) in ICT.

Network and information systems

29 physical servers, 192 TB storage (360 TB backup); 10 Gbps backbone; 650 Mbps international and 6 Gbps domestic Internet; 850 Wi-Fi access points campus-wide, 21 high-speed points at the College; VPN for staff; LMS, ELSE, MyCTU, Google Workspace and Microsoft 365.

Innovation space

The MIS-CTU innovation space on the College of ICT campus, plus self-study and group-work areas that support learning, research and links with industry.

Safety and accessibility

24/7 security, CCTV and annual fire-safety training; new and renovated buildings have ramps, lifts, accessible toilets and signage for people with disabilities.
