Engineer in Computer Networks and Data Communication

Computer Networks and Data Communication is a discipline that studies the principles of computer networks; the ways of designing and building network systems at different levels from local to global; the data exchange between network components; and the development of network applications, web applications, big data processing and high-performance computing.

The programme aims to train engineers with the theoretical, mathematical and scientific foundations used in the computer networking and data communication industries. Students are equipped with basic and in-depth knowledge, the ability to analyse, design and propose solutions that meet the needs of an organisation or individual: network applications, mobile software, free and open-source software, web applications and services, the client-server model, grid computing, cloud computing, big data, parallel and distributed algorithms, and the Internet of Things. Students are able to communicate in both general and specialised English.

High-quality Programme

The high-quality programme trains engineers with strong professional knowledge, professional programming skills and mastery of advanced tools and solutions for network security and high-performance computing. It also provides a solid foundation in mathematics, political and social sciences, law, physical education and national defence education.

Graduates are able to work independently or in teams, meeting the criteria of professional ethics, health and social awareness. They further develop foreign-language competence, in-depth research capability, creativity and an entrepreneurial spirit, meeting the demand of society for high-quality human resources in the context of Industry 4.0.

Engineer in Information Security

The Information Security programme provides students with the capability of protecting digital information, information systems and computer networks; preventing and detecting illegal access, modification, distribution, destruction and attacks on an organisation's information system; proposing, monitoring, assessing and verifying security solutions; and handling incidents related to system security.

Students are equipped with the knowledge and skills required in data privacy, security and integrity, information systems and computer networks; in-depth knowledge of cryptographic techniques, operating system and database security, security of web and Internet applications, security in electronic transactions and e-commerce, network attack and intrusion techniques, protection models and defence techniques against intrusion attacks; the ability to analyse and assess situations; and practice with real-life scenarios for the prevention, detection and blocking of system attacks.

Cisco CCNA certification

Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA). The CCNA programme covers the essential knowledge and skills of computer networking technology such as the layers of the OSI model, LAN, WAN, routing, switching and network security fundamentals, enabling learners to design, install, maintain and troubleshoot network systems in Internet/Intranet environments right after graduation.

On completion of the course, learners receive a Certificate of Course Completion issued by the Cisco Networking Academy and are prepared to take the international CCNA (200-301) examination of Cisco Systems Inc. through Pearson VUE authorised test centres.
