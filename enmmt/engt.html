Mission, Vision & Organisation

Direction, organisation and partner network of the Faculty of Computer Networks and Communications.

Mission, vision and core values

The Faculty of Computer Networks and Communications is an academic unit of the College of Information and Communication Technology (CICT), Can Tho University. The objectives and learning outcomes of its programmes are aligned with the mission and vision of the College and the University.

Mission

Education, scientific research and technology transfer in information and communication technology and related fields, serving the industrialisation and modernisation of the country.

Vision to 2030

CICT becomes a strong and reputable institution in information and communication technology in Asia, meeting international standards of quality assurance in education.

Core values

Consensus – Devotion – Standards – Creativity.

Educational philosophy

CICT: “Thinking, Creativity, Learner-centredness”.
Can Tho University: “Community – Comprehensiveness – Excellence”.

Source: Mission and vision — CICT (in Vietnamese); resolution of the Can Tho University Council (March 2023) on the educational philosophy. How the philosophy is put into practice: Teaching, learning and assessment.
