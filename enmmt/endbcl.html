Cycle II self-assessment (2026)

The self-assessment against AUN-QA version 4.0 (8 criteria, 53 requirements) is part of Can Tho University's programme accreditation plan for 2025–2030. The self-assessment body was established by Decision No. 3832/QĐ-ĐHCT of 16 July 2026, with a coordinating committee, a taskforce led by the Dean, a secretariat and representatives of the functional units. The report was prepared over 9–12 months following the PDCA cycle:

  1. PlanShare the objectives, assign criteria groups and review the recommendations of the 2021 assessment.
  2. DoCollect data and evidence from the Faculty, College, university units, lecturers, students, alumni and employers; gap analysis.
  3. CheckVerify the accuracy of data, the consistency of evidence and progress on improvements.
  4. ActFinalise the report and build the improvement actions into the quality enhancement plan.

2021 AUN-QA recommendations and improvements

The Faculty tracks the groups of recommendations from the 2021 assessment through a corrective plan and progress reports:

Stakeholder feedback

Every semester

Students evaluate every course online; results go to the lecturer and are discussed in Faculty meetings.

At graduation

Students evaluate their whole study experience and their attainment of the learning outcomes.

Every year

Surveys of alumni, employers, lecturers, support staff and graduate employment.

During internships

Host organisations assess students and comment on the programme.

Survey results are published on the Programme outcomes page and used to review the curriculum, teaching and assessment. Feedback to the Faculty is welcome at lt_mmt_tt@cit.ctu.edu.vn or through the feedback link in the footer.

Quality enhancement plan 2026–2028

  • Set attainment thresholds and aggregation rules, and measure a first full cycle under the 10-outcome framework (2026–2028).
  • Extend rubrics to foundation courses; issue a rule for reconciling divergent committee marks (2026–2027).
  • Report appeals, mark changes, processing times and on-time release of marks by semester.
  • Develop a common tool for non-cognitive skills in courses with teamwork and presentations (2027–2028).
  • More practical work, real projects and industry experts; update AI, cloud, big data and cybersecurity content.
  • Strengthen foreign language, communication and problem solving; link student research to lecturers' research.
  • Early warning and study-plan advice for students at risk of delay from the second and third year.
  • Programme-level breakdown of satisfaction surveys; improve learning facilities towards at least 80% satisfaction.
