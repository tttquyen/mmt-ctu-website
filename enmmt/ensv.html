Academic advising

Each class has an academic staff member as its advisor, who supports students with course registration, with planning the whole programme and with any difficulty that arises. Students should meet their advisor before each registration period, and immediately if they receive an academic warning.

The research path

  1. Years 1 – 2Get to know the clubs, seminars and the Science Café of the College.
  2. Years 2 – 3Basic project (from 90 credits earned) and joining a research group.
  3. Years 3 – 4Advanced project (from 110 credits) and a student research topic.
  4. Years 4 – 4.5Thesis or company internship (from 125 credits) and writing a research paper.

Academic staff encourage students to join research topics, write reports and aim for publication at international conferences and in international journals. See also the research activities of the Faculty.

International exchange and study

Students of the high-quality programme take more than 60% of their courses in English and have the opportunity to study abroad. The Faculty and the College have organised short-term student exchanges in Malaysia, Taiwan, Japan and South Korea, most recently at KAIST (2023), DGIST (2024) and KENTECH (2025). Each year 50–100 students of the College receive scholarships for short study visits abroad. With their language and professional skills, graduates are well prepared for postgraduate study at leading universities in Vietnam and abroad.

Company internship

The final semester of all three programmes is dedicated to a company internship. Students work directly at a company and are assessed jointly by the company and an academic supervisor. To register, a student must have earned at least 125 credits and completed the prerequisite courses.

  • A 5-credit course (150 hours): students work full-time for 2–3 months in an organisation and are helped to choose a host that fits their career plans.
  • The company supervisor awards 6 of 10 points on an evaluation form, the academic supervisor the rest; students keep a weekly log.
  • The College's IT Job Fair runs twice a year: 11 fairs from April 2021 to April 2026, with 185 company participations.

The College has cooperation agreements with more than 50 companies in Vietnam and abroad. Selected industry partners: TMA Solutions, IBM Vietnam, Axon Active, Global CyberSoft, FPT Software, Mekong Soft, Harvey Nash, Renesas; abroad, AnyConnect, Expara and BE Solutions (Singapore). See also partners of the College and the Faculty.

Skills to build, difficulties to expect

Seven skills our students should start building in the first year: self-study, technical English, teamwork, time management, programming and Linux, presentation, and finding literature.

Losing direction after high school

Plan each semester; ask the academic advisor and students from earlier intakes.

Overload in the first courses

Do not over-register; focus on mathematics and programming; keep the grade average above 2.00.

Living away from home, financial pressure

Apply for scholarships and student loans; take only part-time work that does not clash with classes.

Social media and games

Set fixed study hours and join a club to find people to study with.
