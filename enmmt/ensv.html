International exchange

Students of the high-quality programme take more than 60% of their courses in English and have the opportunity to study abroad. With strong language and professional skills, graduates are well prepared for postgraduate study at leading universities at home and abroad.

Company internship

The ninth semester of all three programmes is dedicated to a company internship. Students work directly at a company and are assessed jointly by the company and an academic supervisor.
