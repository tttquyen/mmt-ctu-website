Our people

A highly qualified academic staff of 02 Associate Professors, 07 PhDs, 08 Masters and 01 Engineer, most of whom graduated from countries with advanced education systems.

The Faculty is in charge of undergraduate programmes in the field of computer networks and communications. Faculty members also assist in teaching, appraising and evaluating training programmes, and supervising master's students and PhD candidates at other universities in Can Tho and the provinces of the Mekong Delta.
