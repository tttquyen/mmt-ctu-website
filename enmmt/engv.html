Our people

A highly qualified academic staff of 02 Associate Professors, 07 PhDs, 08 Masters and 01 Engineer, most of whom graduated from countries with advanced education systems.

The Faculty is in charge of undergraduate programmes in the field of computer networks and communications. Faculty members also assist in teaching, appraising and evaluating training programmes, and supervising master's students and PhD candidates at other universities in Can Tho and the provinces of the Mekong Delta.

Staff development

Staff size and qualifications, 2022–2026

Over five years the Faculty grew from 14 to 18 staff, and lecturers holding a doctorate increased from 4 to 9 (from 28.57% to 50%). All staff are full-time. Three lecturers are currently doing a PhD and one staff member a master's degree. Lecturers are recruited with at least a master's degree in a relevant field, English at B1 level or above, and a commitment to complete a PhD.

Workload is monitored every year in standard hours of teaching, research and community service, together with staff FTE and the student–staff ratio. Recent professional development: in 2024, 10 lecturers and assistants completed Malware Analysis and Network Forensics at Asia Pacific University (Malaysia); in 2025, two staff took part in a live cyber-defence exercise run by the Can Tho City Police; in 2026, lecturers attended a seminar on source-code vulnerability detection at the College.
