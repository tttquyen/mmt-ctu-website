Computer Networks, Mobile Communications & Big Data Lab

Head of laboratory: Assoc. Prof. Dr. Do Thanh Nghi

Development orientation

The laboratory focuses on three main areas: computer networks, high-performance computing and data science.

The College of ICT has invested seriously in both facilities and research staff. In return, the laboratory has contributed numerous ISI-ranked international publications, projects and scholarships, and in particular has improved teaching so that it keeps pace with new research directions and technologies.

Cloud computing infrastructure

The laboratory has successfully deployed the private cloud of the College of ICT (cloud.cit.ctu.edu.vn/horizon), bringing considerable benefits to the teaching activities of the College and of Can Tho University.

Cloud computing is a comprehensive information technology solution that uses computing technologies and servers developed over the Internet. It creates a virtual working environment that helps organisations provide resources flexibly while significantly reducing investment, maintenance and energy costs.

Research cooperation

The laboratory has carried out cooperative projects both in Vietnam and abroad, with An Giang province, Can Tho City, Tien Giang province, Blaise Pascal University (France), Université du Québec (Canada) and Université de Rennes 1 (France), among others. It also helps introduce PhD and master's scholarships to lecturers and students.
