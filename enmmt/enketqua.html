Programme Outcomes

Data on student achievement, graduation and employability, together with research output — updated for each academic year.

The Faculty monitors, by cohort and by year, the key outcome indicators of the programme: graduation and drop-out rates, time to graduation, graduate employment, attainment of the programme learning outcomes and stakeholder satisfaction. The results feed into programme review and improvement.

Figures refer to the Computer Networks and Data Communication programme (standard track), as compiled in the programme's AUN-QA self-assessment report, 2026.

Graduation and drop-out rates

The designed duration is 9 main semesters (4.5 years). Over the five most recent cohorts the graduation rate was 68.03% – 70.79% and the average time to graduation 8.84 – 9.73 semesters. Cohort 47 had the shortest average time (8.84 semesters), with 66.3% of students graduating within 9 semesters.

Percentages of each cohort's intake; the remainder are students still enrolled.

76.5% of drop-outs and transfers occur from the third year onwards. Delays are mainly due to registering fewer credits than the model study plan, absence from or failing courses, and not yet meeting the foreign-language requirement. Following the 2023 workshop on on-time graduation, the Faculty strengthened academic advising, tracks credit accumulation with its own progress-monitoring system and reminds students to meet the language requirement early.

Employability

Can Tho University surveys graduate employment every year. Over five years, an average of 94.8% of respondents were employed or in further study.

Time to employment, sector and relevance

Sector and relevance are counted over employed graduates who responded.

Over five years the private sector employed 78.8% of working graduates, the public sector 13.9%, and 7.3% were self-employed; about 78.8% of jobs were in or related to the field of study. In 2024 graduates took longer to find work, in line with the trend across the discipline group. The College holds an IT Job Fair twice a year: 11 fairs from April 2021 to April 2026, with 13–22 companies each and 185 company participations in total.

Attainment of learning outcomes

Attainment is measured with both direct and indirect data:

  • Direct measurement. The university's academic management system computes each student's score on each learning outcome from the courses mapped to it. For 60 students of two Cohort 47 classes graduating between semester 3 of 2024–2025 and semester 3 of 2025–2026, 14 of the 15 outcomes were attained by more than 80% of students at a threshold of 5.0/10. The foreign-language outcome shows only 20/60, because students who met it by certificate have no course mark to convert.
  • Internship supervisors. Company supervisors award 6 of the 10 points of the Company Internship course.
  • Graduate and employer surveys on a five-point scale (table below).

Employers rated the autonomy and responsibility outcomes highest (4.42/5; 93.8% at level 4–5). Foreign language scored lower than the other outcomes (3.99/5) in both surveys; lecturers have been encouraged to use more English materials and English presentations and reports.

Cohorts graduating so far studied under the 15-outcome framework. The 10-outcome framework applied from Cohort 51 (expected to graduate in 2030) will be measured over a full cycle under the quality improvement plan.

Stakeholder satisfaction

Can Tho University surveys students, graduating students, alumni, lecturers, support staff and employers every year.

Share of “satisfied” and “very satisfied”. In 2024 the graduating-student survey was broadened to the whole study experience and is not directly comparable.

In 2025 employers were satisfied with graduates' professional knowledge (100%), professional skills (87.5%) and autonomy and discipline (87.5%); 91.7% of alumni agreed that their studies built lifelong-learning capacity. Areas for continued improvement are foreign language, communication and problem-solving skills, research support and practical internships.

Research output

Student academic and international activities

  • The CICT CPC programming contest, the Vietnam National Olympiad in Informatics for students, cybersecurity contests and hackathons in Vietnam and at Asia Pacific University (Malaysia).
  • The ECCIT English Club, with 18 English-language and international activities in 2022–2026; the ECCIT Talents contest has run every year from 2022 to 2025.
  • Academic exchanges in Malaysia and Korea: KAIST (2023), DGIST (2024), KENTECH (2025). Each year 50–100 students of the College receive scholarships for short study visits abroad.
