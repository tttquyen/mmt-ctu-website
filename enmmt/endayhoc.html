Teaching and learning activities

The programmes combine lectures with a substantial amount of practical work. The following learning activities are used across all three programmes:

Laboratory practice

Courses on computer networks, network management, network security and cloud computing are practised on the equipment and infrastructure of the Faculty's two laboratories.

Projects at increasing levels

A basic project in year three and an advanced project in year four gradually introduce students to research and to solving real-world problems.

Company internship

The ninth semester is dedicated to an internship — students work in a real professional environment before graduation.

Graduation thesis / project

Students carry out a topic under the supervision of an academic staff member and defend it before a committee.

Students of the high-quality programme take more than 60% of their courses in English and have the opportunity to join exchange and study programmes abroad.
