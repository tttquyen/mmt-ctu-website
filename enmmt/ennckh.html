Scientific research

131 publications between 2022 and 2026, 9 faculty research projects, 6 student research projects, together with textbooks and technology-transfer products.

Our members are actively involved in scientific research with significant sources of research funding both on and off campus.

Faculty members have experience in leading, participating in, consulting on, implementing and reviewing research projects at institutional, municipal and national levels, many of which have been applied in practice. Faculty members and students also contribute through books, book chapters and articles published in prestigious journals and conferences indexed by ISI/Scopus and listed by the State Council for Professorship.

Publications, 2022 — 2026

Between 2022 and 2026, Faculty members published 131 research works, of which 101 (77.1%) appeared in international venues and 30 in domestic ones. Output grew steadily across the complete years: from 18 works in 2022 to 35 in 2025 — a 94.4% increase over four years, averaging 29.3 works per year. In the first eight months of 2026 alone there were 14 works, 13 of them international.

Research collaboration

Among the 131 publications, 9 have co-authors from foreign institutions in France (Université d’Artois, Université de Montpellier, Université de Lorraine), Morocco and South Korea.
